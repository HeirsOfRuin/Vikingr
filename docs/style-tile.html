What changes from Phase 1

  • One line of status at the top. Crew, wounds and the legends table move into sheets you pull up when you want them.
  • The scene band leads every important moment. Here it is drawn in code; in the game it will be your source image converted to this palette, with the fire, smoke and figures animated over it.
  • Choices are plain text with a red edge on focus. Odds and requirements are small pills. A lethal choice carries ᛦ, the rune later rune-rows used for death.
  • Tabs get pixel icons in the same palette: a torch, a ship, a helmet, a shield-row, a runestone.
  • Gold appears only where silver and fame are. Red is reserved for headings, danger and what you are about to do.

The fire

The hall fire is a 192 × 96 pixel scene. The flames are computed each frame, the light around the hearth pulses in three hard bands so the pixels stay clean, and embers and smoke rise to the roof-hole. It runs at 12 frames a second and holds still for anyone who has reduced motion turned on.

For your verdict

  1. The palette and the proportion: stone first, bone text, red where the eye should go, gold rarely.
  2. The type: Alegreya for prose and headings, Alegreya Sans for the interface.
  3. The density of the game screen: one status line, the scene band, prose, choices, tabs.
  4. The knotwork at the top is an ornament prototype. Urnes style, two thick strands and one thin. It frames titles, scene art and the saga only.

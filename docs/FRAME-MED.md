# The Middle Sea: Hastein and Björn, 859–862 (step 3 of the places plan)

Josh agreed the order on 2026-10-28 (the thin places, then the East road, then this). On 2026-10-30 he said to frame it while he playtests; the build waits for his notes.

**Where it stands today:**
- The chronicle has the voyage as news: c859_voyage, c860_luna and c862_return.
- Hastein and Björn are in the legends table.
- No life can sail with them.

## Why
- **A mid-career great voyage for an 843 life,** which runs out of Loire story by about 850. In 859 an 843 hero is 32: the right age to be a captain in the fleet, not an oarsman in it.
- **The richest and deadliest venture in the game, by the sources:**
  - 62 ships went out;
  - about 20 came back, three years later;
  - they had sacked towns on three coasts, ransomed a king, and burned the mosque at Algeciras.
- **It reuses what exists:**
  - the great fleet;
  - living off the land (`days_foraged`);
  - the far winter (`winter_far`, from the East road);
  - host battles, captives and goods.

## Sources
**Contemporary, or close:**
- **Annals of St-Bertin (859–862):**
  - the fleet past Spain;
  - the winter in the Camargue;
  - the Rhône raided up to Valence;
  - beaten by Count Gerard, the fleet turned to Italy.
- **Ibn Idhari and Ibn Hayyan, from the Córdoba records:**
  - the 62 ships;
  - Algeciras' mosque burned;
  - Nekor in Morocco sacked;
  - the Balearics raided;
  - the emir's fleet catching them in the Strait on the way home, with heavy losses;
  - Pamplona, where García Íñiguez, king of the Navarrese, was taken and ransomed for 70,000 dinars.
- **The Chronicle of Alfonso III:** Galicia's coast guard beat them off.

**Late, told only as the men's stories:**
- **Dudo of Saint-Quentin (c. 1015):**
  - Hastein took Luna thinking it was Rome;
  - he got inside by playing dead, with a funeral procession into the town's church.
- **The Irish Fragmentary Annals:** "blue men" (Africans) brought back to Ireland.
- **The idea that the voyage was Björn's,** which comes from the sagas.

## Rules
- **Who can go:** an own ship, and in Noirmoutier, Nantes or the Loire in spring 859. 830 lives (aged 45) can join too.
- **Leaving:** the fleet is Hastein's (`fleet: med859`, until 862). A captain can leave after the Rhône or the second winter, at the cost of fame and Hastein's favour.
- **Time:** two winters away. The farm, the family and the hardship at home run without you, as on the East road.
- **The bot:** a captain-type bot joins when it can, so the voyage is measured.

## Assumptions and risks
- **The Muslim-world scenes need care.** Algeciras' mosque and Nekor's captives are what happened. They are told plainly, with the people there as people, under the style guide's rules on captives and on violence, never as spectacle.
- **Dudo's Luna story is almost certainly legend.** It's told as Hastein's own boast, which the men repeat.
- **Two winters away is long,** but it is the point of the voyage.

# Vikingr is out: a life in the Viking age, with the first two years free

Vikingr is the biggest thing I have made. It is one man's life in the age of the great raids, 830 to 878: you start rowing in another captain's ship, and what follows is up to you. Iona and Bangor, the beaches of Kaupang and Hedeby, the Irish ship-forts, the Frankish rivers, and in time the Great Army in England. It is a story told in short scenes with choices, with a strategy game underneath: men to feed and pay, ships to buy and mend, shield-walls and single combat, lords to serve or defy, land to take and hold, and a son to carry the saga on when you die.

## The demo is free

The demo is the first two years, 830 and 831, played right here in the browser. When it ends, at the spring muster of 832, the game gives you a save code. Load it into the full game and your life carries on from the same spring, with everything you did.

## The full game

- 646 written scenes, about 69,000 words, over 1,500 choices.
- 56 places to sail to, from Iceland to Garðaríki.
- Three starting eras: 830, 843 and the Great Army of 865.
- Three backgrounds, six upbringings, thirteen historical rivals.
- A download that plays offline in any browser, and an Android app.

It is $5, and you can pay more if you want to. For the first week it is 20% off.
